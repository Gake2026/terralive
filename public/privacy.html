Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

TerraLive ("the service") shows live public data - aircraft positions, ship positions, satellite orbits, and earthquakes - on an interactive globe. This policy explains what information is and is not handled when you use it.

Information we do not collect

  • No accounts, no sign-up, and no personal details such as your name or email address.
  • No tracking cookies and no third-party analytics on this site.
  • We do not build profiles of visitors and we do not sell any data, because there is none to sell.

Information handled automatically

Like almost every website, our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (for example, IP address, user agent, and requested pages) for operating and securing the service. These logs are kept briefly and are not combined with anything else.

Your browser also loads map tiles directly from our tile provider (Esri) and live data through our own cache server, which in turn reads public feeds (including adsb.lol, CelesTrak, the USGS, and Digitraffic). We do not send those providers any personal information about you.

Data shown on the globe

Everything displayed - flight tracks, vessel positions, satellite orbits, earthquake reports - comes from publicly available sources. It can be delayed, incomplete, or wrong. It is shown for general interest only.

Children

The service is a general-audience website and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
